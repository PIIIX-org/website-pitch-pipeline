[Navigation]           -> Brand Logo | Heating | Commercial HVAC | Maintenance Plans | Instant Quote
[Hero Section]         -> "Engineered for Wyoming Winters" + "Turn to Arrowhead in Casper & Douglas"

[Commercial HVAC]      -> Rooftop Units (RTUs), Boilers, Warehouses, Retail & Office Facilities

[Footer]               -> Casper & Douglas Service Area · Direct Phone · Public Contact

## 2. Interactive Flow
1. **Emergency Path:** Red/Amber flashing beacon badge with one-click direct phone dial for frozen pipes / furnace shutdown.
2. **Commercial Bid Path:** Facility managers select building square footage ($< 5,000$ sq ft, $5,000 - 25,000$ sq ft, $> 25,000$ sq ft) to calculate estimated seasonal filter & burner tune-up cadence.
