# Brand Guide: Arrowhead Heating & Air Conditioning (Wyoming)

## 1. Color Direction (Eliminating Baby Pink & Neon Green)

| Role | Color Name | Hex | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dominant Dark** | Carbon Slate | `#0B0F19` | Deep, rugged background conveying authority and night dispatch |
| **Surface Dark** | Gunmetal Gray | `#1E293B` | Card backgrounds, service containers, quote modules |
| **Accent Primary** | Wyoming Heat Amber | `#F97316` | Emergency CTA, tap-to-call badges, heating heat indicator |
| **Accent Secondary** | Industrial Frost | `#38BDF8` | Cooling indicators, refrigeration services |
| **Text Main** | Crisp Winter White | `#F8FAFC` | Headlines, primary text, high contrast readability |
| **Text Muted** | Steel Silver | `#94A3B8` | Subtext, specifications, licensing info |

## 2. Typography
- **Headlines:** `Space Grotesk` or bold industrial sans-serif (heavyweight, technical, clean).
- **Body:** `Inter` (neutral, crisp, easily readable on mobile devices in sub-zero gloves).

## 3. Imagery Logic
- Real commercial rooftop units (RTUs), industrial boilers, and furnace installations.
- Snowy Wyoming mountain ranges and Casper Mountain backdrop.
- NO teapots, NO pastel knit blankets.
