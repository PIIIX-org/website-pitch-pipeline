# Design & Pitch Brief: Arrowhead Heating & Air Conditioning

- **Business:** Arrowhead Heating & Air Conditioning
- **Target Geography:** Casper & Douglas, Wyoming
- **Industry:** Residential & Commercial HVAC, Heating, Boiler Maintenance
- **Current Website:** `http://arrowheadhvacr.com`
- **Phone:** `(307) 266-2177`

---

## 1. The Real-World Context: "Winter is Coming in Wyoming"
In Casper and Douglas, winter temperatures regularly plummet below **-15°F (-26°C)** with blizzard conditions and fierce prairie winds. Heating failure in a commercial building (warehouse, office, clinic, retail) or residential home is not just uncomfortable—it causes burst frozen pipes and catastrophic structural damage within hours.

## 2. Weaknesses Countered
- **Current Problem 1 (Security & Insecure Warning):** Site is on HTTP (no HTTPS). Modern browsers flash a red "Not Secure" warning in the URL bar, instantly destroying trust when a business owner seeks a high-ticket commercial contractor.
- **Current Problem 2 (Dead Google+ Social Link):** Header still prominently features the Google+ `G+` icon (discontinued in 2019). Tells sophisticated buyers the site hasn't been maintained in 7+ years.
- **Current Problem 3 (Palette & Imagery Mismatch):** Baby pink navigation header (`#f8a5c2`) clashing with bright neon lime green headlines, overlaid on a stock photo of a steaming teapot on a coffee table. Looks like an artisanal afternoon tea cafe rather than a rugged Wyoming heating contractor.
- **Current Problem 4 (No Commercial Preventative Tiers):** Lacks clear commercial maintenance agreement breakdowns for building managers.

## 3. The New Pitch Angle & Positioning
**"Engineered for Wyoming Winters. Trusted in Casper & Douglas."**
- Immediate high-contrast emergency dispatcher bar ("Furnace Down? Emergency Dispatch 24/7 in Casper & Douglas").
- Industrial, rugged visual tone: Deep carbon slate (`#0B0F19`), high-visibility safety amber (`#F59E0B`), and crisp winter steel (`#E2E8F0`).
- Commercial Preventative Maintenance Calculator: Helps property managers calculate annual furnace & boiler tune-up savings before winter freezes strike.
